About Us

Emprada is a globally diversified leader specialising in natural resources and trading.

We add value to physical commodities ethically and responsibly. Our approach covers the whole supply chain — sourcing and mining, processing, storage, transport and delivery — so products reach markets where they are needed.

Guided by integrity, our team builds lasting relationships with customers, suppliers and partners through trust, operational excellence and responsible stewardship.

About Us

At Emprada, we specialise in moving physical commodities from production centres to where they are most needed.

Prosperity and trade go hand in hand — and that depends on managing trade flows smoothly from origin to destination. Our platform is built to support international trade and keep improving supply-chain efficiency.

We keep adding capability across business verticals and connecting them to our core operations for scale and diversity. Investing in local resources for a global market, we put responsible sourcing, ethical trade, safety and sustainability first.
