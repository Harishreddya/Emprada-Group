Overview

Direct from the world’s single largest barite mine.

Sourcing directly from a Govt. of Andhra Pradesh mine spread over 225 hectares, Emprada supplies high-quality barite powder — up to 96% BaSO₄, free of toxic materials — to clients across the globe.
